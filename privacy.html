Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

1. General provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) sets out how information of users (the “User”) of the BudgetBull mobile application (the “App”) is processed.

1.2. The App is intended for personal finance tracking. The App does not provide for user accounts, does not use servers operated by the Developer (other than the service receiving bug reports and feedback, clause 4.11), and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Information entered by the User is stored exclusively on the User’s device and is not transmitted to the Developer, except for information the User voluntarily sends in a bug report.

1.3. The Developer does not sell or share User information with third parties, does not use it for advertising purposes and does not track the User within the meaning of App Tracking Transparency.

2. Developer

2.1. Developer of the App: Vitalii Prokopenko. Email address: —.

2.2. With respect to information stored on the User’s device, the Developer is not a controller within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), as the Developer does not receive, store or process such information.

3. Information stored on the device

3.1. Transactions, categories, budgets, goals, events, debt obligations, tax parameters, asset records and App settings are stored in the App’s local storage on the User’s device.

3.2. The personal monobank token and price-feed API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, accessible only on that device and only while it is unlocked. Such data is not included in backups or export files.

3.3. Transaction data received through a Wallet automation in the Shortcuts app is stored in the App’s local storage until processed by the User.

3.4. Receipt images are processed on the device by Apple frameworks (VisionKit, Vision and, where available, Apple Intelligence with an on-device language model). The App retains only the fields confirmed by the User; the image is neither stored nor transmitted.

4. Disclosure to third parties

4.1. The App sends requests only to the third-party services listed below and only when the User uses the corresponding features. Each request discloses to the service the User’s IP address and the technical parameters of the request, which the service processes under its own privacy policy. No such data is transmitted to the Developer.

4.2. monobank (JSC Universal Bank) — where the User connects a personal token. The App requests account and statement data directly from the bank. The token is issued and revoked by the User at api.monobank.ua.

4.3. The price feeds CoinMarketCap, Twelve Data and Finnhub — where the User provides their own key. The key and the tickers or identifiers of assets are transmitted. The quantity and value of the User’s holdings are not transmitted.

4.4. The logo services Elbstream and jsDelivr (cryptocurrency icons). The request contains the asset’s ticker.

4.5. Central banks — to obtain official exchange rates: the bank of the User’s country of tax residence, then the bank of the App currency, then the European Central Bank (data-api.ecb.europa.eu) and the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua); for residents of Poland, Narodowy Bank Polski (api.nbp.pl). Official prices of precious metals come from the National Bank of Ukraine. The request contains only a date and a currency code and no personal data.

4.6. Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation) — to obtain a description of a merchant when a receipt is scanned. Only the merchant’s name is transmitted; the amount, the items purchased and any information about the User are not.

4.7. Certain services listed in this section are located outside the European Economic Area and process the IP address under safeguards they determine themselves. Such requests are made by the User directly; the Developer is not a party to them.

4.8. OpenRouter (OpenRouter, Inc.) and the language model providers to which OpenRouter routes a request — where the User enables the Bu AI feature (the App’s AI assistant) with their own OpenRouter account. The following are transmitted: the User’s questions and messages; a system instruction (current date, currency, list of categories and income sources); data the App provides to the model at its request in order to answer or perform an action — aggregate figures and individual records (transactions with date, amount, category and merchant, goals, events, debts, assets); the User’s ratings of earlier answers together with excerpts of the related questions and answers. Receipt images and tokens or keys for other services are not transmitted. Certain free models may retain requests and use them for training under the terms of their providers.

4.9. Data is transmitted to OpenRouter only after the User’s explicit consent given in the App (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Consent may be withdrawn at any time in the Bu AI settings, whereupon the OpenRouter key, chat history and answer ratings are deleted from the device and transmission ceases. Chat history (up to the 100 most recent messages) and answer ratings are kept in the App’s local storage until deleted by the User, consent is withdrawn or all data is deleted. Sign-in to OpenRouter takes place in the system authentication window; the return to the App passes through the page budgetbull.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives a one-time authorisation code and the IP address and does not store them.

4.10. OpenRouter, Inc., the language model providers and GitHub, Inc. are independent third parties. The Developer is not affiliated with them, is not a party to the agreement between the User and OpenRouter, and does not control their processing of data, which is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

4.11. Bug reports and feedback the User sends through the Report a problem and Send feedback forms are received by a server operated for the Developer on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.), which stores them and posts a notification to the Developer’s private Telegram channel. The following are transmitted: the text of the message; a rating (for feedback); files attached by the User (up to 5): metadata (including location, device model and capture date) is removed from images and videos before sending; other files, and files that could not be cleaned, are sent unchanged; with the User’s consent, technical details (App and iOS version, language) and, when sent from Bu AI, the question, the answer and the Bu AI action log, including any financial information already contained in that chat. Before sending, the User sees the entire content of the report and may switch the technical details off. Sending is based on the User’s consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Reports are used solely to correct errors and develop the App. They are stored in the Developer’s database on the Cloudflare platform (Cloudflare D1) for no longer than 24 months from the date they are sent and are then deleted automatically; the copies in the Telegram channel, including attachments, are deleted automatically within 12 months. To group similar reports and prepare summaries for the Developer, the text of a report is processed by Cloudflare’s artificial intelligence models (Workers AI) on Cloudflare’s infrastructure, without being passed to anyone else. The User may delete a sent report at any time under More → Send feedback → Sent; its copy in the Telegram channel is removed at the same time if no more than 48 hours have passed since sending, and otherwise automatically within the period stated above. For this, the App keeps a random deletion key only on the User’s device, and the Developer keeps only its hash. A report may also be deleted at the User’s request quoting its number. Cloudflare, Inc. and Telegram are independent third parties and process the IP address and the content of the report while transmitting and storing it under their own policies. Where this channel is unavailable or not chosen, the report is sent by email or through the system Share sheet.

5. iOS features

5.1. Face ID and passcode verification is performed by iOS. The App receives only the result of verification and has no access to biometric data.

5.2. Reminders are implemented as local notifications generated on the device. No push notification server is used.

5.3. Payment details (amount, merchant, card) are provided to the App by iOS on the device through an automation configured by the User in the Shortcuts app.

5.4. Backups are written to a folder selected by the User in the Files app (including iCloud Drive). Storage of backups is governed by the terms of Apple or another storage provider. Backup files are not encrypted by the App; the User is responsible for where they are kept.

5.5. CSV export is performed through the standard system Share sheet; the recipient is determined by the User.

6. Apple diagnostics and TestFlight

6.1. Where “Share With App Developers” is enabled in iOS settings, Apple provides the Developer with anonymised crash reports and aggregate usage statistics. Such data is used solely to correct errors.

6.2. Where the User participates in testing through TestFlight, Apple provides the Developer with the data transmitted by TestFlight: the tester’s name and email address, feedback, screenshots and crash reports. Such data is used solely for testing purposes and is not disclosed to third parties.

6.3. Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement permits developers to collect technical data about the device. Except as set out in this section, the Developer does not exercise that right.

7. Correspondence

7.1. When contacted by email, the Developer receives the sender’s address and the content of the message. Such data is used solely to respond and is deleted no later than 24 months after the correspondence ends.

8. Legal basis for processing

8.1. Processing on the device is carried out to perform the features used by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Requests to third-party services are sent only on the User’s initiative and cease when the corresponding feature is disabled.

8.2. Apple diagnostics and User correspondence are processed on the basis of the Developer’s legitimate interest in correcting errors and responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Retention and deletion

9.1. Information is stored on the device for as long as the App is installed and is deleted together with it.

9.2. Data in the iOS Keychain may persist after the App is deleted. Before deleting the App, the User is advised to use More → Delete all my data.

9.3. Backups are deleted by the User in the Files app.

9.4. The User may revoke the monobank token at any time at api.monobank.ua.

9.5. The Delete all my data function removes all App data from the device, including data in the iOS Keychain, Bu AI history and scheduled notifications. Backups in the folder selected by the User are not deleted.

10. User rights

10.1. The User may at any time view, rectify, export (CSV, backup) and delete their information by means of the App. The Developer holds no copy of the information stored in the App. Requests for access to or deletion of correspondence, bug reports and TestFlight data shall be sent to the email address stated in Section 2. The User has the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of their country.

11. Security

11.1. Confidential data is stored in the iOS Keychain; network requests are made over HTTPS; access to the App may be restricted by Face ID or passcode (More → Security). The protection of information on the device also depends on the device passcode.

11.2. The Developer holds no information from the App other than bug reports voluntarily sent; accordingly, an incident on the Developer’s side cannot result in its disclosure. The safety of information depends on the protection of the User’s device and of the locations where backups are kept.

12. Minors

12.1. The App is not intended for persons under the age of 16.

13. Amendments

13.1. The Developer may amend this Policy. The revised version is published in the App and on the Policy page together with its effective date. The User is notified in the App of any amendment affecting the transmission of information off the device.
