Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

1. General provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) sets out how information of users (the “User”) of the BudgetBull mobile application (the “App”) is processed.

1.2. The App is intended for personal finance tracking. No account is required: the User may optionally sign in with Apple or Google (clauses 4.12 and 4.13). The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Information entered by the User is stored on the User’s device and, with iCloud sync, also in the User’s private iCloud database, in encrypted form only (clause 5.6). The Developer’s servers are used for three functions only: Bu, the in-app AI assistant (clause 4.8), bug reports and feedback (clause 4.11), and revoking Sign in with Apple (clause 4.12). Information leaves the device only as described in Sections 4 to 6.

1.3. The Developer does not sell User information, does not use it for advertising purposes and does not track the User within the meaning of App Tracking Transparency. Information is disclosed only to the services named in Sections 4 to 6, to the extent and for the purposes stated there.

2. Developer

2.1. Developer of the App: Vitalii Prokopenko. Postal address: ul. Podwisłocze 31/104, 35-309 Rzeszów, Polska. Email address: budgetbull.support@gmail.com.

2.2. With respect to information stored on the User’s device and in the User’s private iCloud database, the Developer is not a controller within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), as the Developer does not receive that information. With respect to data processed by the Developer’s servers (clauses 4.8, 4.11 and 4.12), the Developer is the controller; Cloudflare, Inc. and Anthropic, PBC process such data on the Developer’s behalf as processors (clause 4.10).

3. Information stored on the device

3.1. Transactions, categories, budgets, goals, events, debt obligations, tax parameters, asset records and App settings are stored in the App’s local storage on the User’s device.

3.2. The personal monobank token and price-feed API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, accessible only on that device and only while it is unlocked. Such data is not included in backups or export files. The key that encrypts App data is stored as described in clause 3.5.

3.3. Transaction data received through a Wallet automation in the Shortcuts app is stored in the App’s local storage until processed by the User.

3.4. Receipt images are processed on the device by Apple frameworks (VisionKit, Vision and, where available, Apple Intelligence with an on-device language model). The App retains only the fields confirmed by the User; the image is neither stored nor transmitted.

3.5. The information listed in clause 3.1 (except certain settings: reminders, App lock and appearance) and Bu history are stored on the device in encrypted form (AES-256-GCM, Apple CryptoKit). The encryption key is generated on the device and stored in the iOS Keychain as a synchronised item: when iCloud Keychain is turned on, it is transferred to the User’s other devices with Apple’s end-to-end encryption. The Developer does not receive the encryption key. A backup password, if set by the User, is stored neither on the device nor in the backups: they hold only a copy of the encryption key, encrypted with a key derived from that password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600,000 iterations).

4. Disclosure to third parties

4.1. The App sends requests to the third-party services listed in clauses 4.2 to 4.6 directly from the device and only when the User uses the corresponding features. Each request discloses to the service the User’s IP address and the technical parameters of the request, which the service processes under its own privacy policy. No such data is transmitted to the Developer.

4.2. monobank (JSC Universal Bank) — where the User connects a personal token. The App requests account and statement data directly from the bank. The token is issued and revoked by the User at api.monobank.ua.

4.3. The price feeds CoinMarketCap, Twelve Data and Finnhub — where the User provides their own key. The key and the tickers or identifiers of assets are transmitted. The quantity and value of the User’s holdings are not transmitted. For the net worth history chart, the App additionally obtains daily prices: for cryptocurrencies from Binance’s public API without a key, and for US stocks from Twelve Data with the User’s key; in both cases only the ticker is transmitted. Cryptocurrency prices are also requested from Binance (data-api.binance.vision, with api.binance.com as a fallback) without an account or key; only the ticker is transmitted. Current and daily prices of cryptocurrencies not listed on Binance are likewise requested from Bybit (api.bybit.com, with api.bytick.com as a fallback) and OKX (www.okx.com) without an account or key; only the ticker is transmitted.

4.4. The logo services Elbstream, Logo.dev (bank logos) and jsDelivr (cryptocurrency icons). The request contains the asset’s ticker or the bank’s website address.

4.5. Central banks — to obtain official exchange rates and official prices of precious metals. Exchange rates are requested from the central bank of the User’s country of residence as set in the App, where the App supports that bank: Narodowy Bank Polski (api.nbp.pl) for Poland, the European Central Bank (data-api.ecb.europa.eu) for the euro area, the National Bank of Georgia (nbg.gov.ge) for Georgia, and the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua) solely for Users resident in Ukraine. Otherwise, or where that bank does not publish the required rate, rates are requested from the bank of the display currency, then from the European Central Bank, then from Narodowy Bank Polski for currencies the European Central Bank does not publish, and finally from the National Bank of Georgia (nbg.gov.ge) for the Georgian lari and the Kazakhstani tenge. Prices of precious metals are obtained from the National Bank of Ukraine for Users resident in Ukraine; for all other Users, the price of gold is obtained from Narodowy Bank Polski. The request contains only a date and a currency code and no personal data. In addition, to check the device clock the App uses the Date header of the responses of the same central bank of the User’s country of residence (the European Central Bank where the App does not support that bank) and a separate HEAD request to it, so that a month is not closed on a wrong clock; these requests contain no personal data.

4.6. Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation) — to obtain a description of a merchant when a receipt is scanned. Only the merchant’s name is transmitted; the amount, the items purchased and any information about the User are not.

4.7. Certain services listed in clauses 4.2 to 4.6 are located outside the European Economic Area and process the IP address under safeguards they determine themselves. Such requests are made by the User directly; the Developer is not a party to them.

4.8. The BudgetBull server (a service operated for the Developer on Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare, Inc.) and Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC), the provider of the Claude language model — where the User enables the feature Bu, the in-app AI assistant. The App sends Bu requests to the Developer’s server, which passes them on to Anthropic and returns the answer; no account or key of the User with these services is needed. The following are transmitted: the User’s questions and messages; a system instruction (current date, currency, list of categories and income sources); data the App provides to the model at its request in order to answer or perform an action — aggregate figures and individual records (transactions with date, amount, category and merchant, goals, events, debts, assets); the User’s ratings of earlier answers together with excerpts of the related questions and answers. When the User imports a table, its header row, up to 5 sample rows with names and long numbers hidden, and the list of categories are also transmitted; the App also makes service requests of its own, such as a summary of an older part of the chat, which are not counted as questions. Receipt images and tokens or keys for other services are not transmitted. For operations the App recorded on its own from bank notifications or Wallet and that the User has not yet opened or changed, the merchant and note are not transmitted. Anthropic does not use data sent through its API to train its models. The server does not store the text of questions and answers, amounts or any other content of the chat. To apply the question limits and prevent abuse, the server stores, tied to a random key created on the User’s device by Apple’s App Attest: the public part of that key; counters of questions per month and per day and of refused and service requests; where the User has Plus or its free trial, a hash of Apple’s signed purchase transaction, the plan, the dates of the paid period and the original transaction identifier, which links up to five devices of one subscription; where the User has bought question packs, the identifiers of those App Store transactions, the number of questions bought and the remaining balance. These records are kept while Bu is used on the device and are deleted automatically 24 months after the last request, except a remaining balance of bought questions, which is kept, together with the device key needed to spend it, until it is spent or until the User asks for its deletion (Section 10). In addition, the server keeps a technical log of each request to the model without any text (time, a hash of the device key, the question identifier, plan, number of tokens, cost and outcome) for 30 days, and, for 24 hours, question identifiers with the device key, the number of model calls and how the question was paid. The IP address is used only to limit the frequency of requests and is not stored by the Developer. When Bu is opened, the model also receives the name of the screen it was opened from, the internal identifier of the open record (a transaction, goal, debt or month) and up to the 10 most recent actions in the App — their names only, without amounts or text.

4.9. Data is transmitted to Anthropic only after the User’s explicit consent given in the App (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); where the information transmitted to Bu changes, the App asks for consent again. Consent may be withdrawn at any time by turning Bu off in its settings, whereupon chat history and answer ratings are deleted from the device and transmission ceases. The server records described in clause 4.8 are processed to provide the questions included in the User’s plan or bought by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to protect the service against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Chat history (up to the 100 most recent messages) and answer ratings are kept in the App’s local storage until deleted by the User, consent is withdrawn or all data is deleted. The short notes Bu keeps about each chat and the facts the User saves under About me are stored with the chat history on the device in encrypted form, are included in backups but not in iCloud sync, and are transmitted to Anthropic only together with a question or in the service request that updates a chat’s notes (clause 4.8); deleting a chat deletes its notes, the User may delete each fact, and turning Bu off deletes all of them.

4.10. Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) and Anthropic, PBC (USA) process data on the Developer’s behalf as processors under their terms for business customers, which include data processing agreements. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States; such transfers are based on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission included in those agreements and, where the recipient is certified, on the EU‑U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Anthropic keeps API requests only for the limited period set out in its policies, which may be longer where needed to enforce its usage policy. The Developer is not affiliated with Anthropic or Cloudflare.

4.11. Bug reports and feedback the User sends through the Write to me form (including from Bu and after shaking the phone) and the app rating form are received by a server operated for the Developer on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.), which stores them and posts a notification to the Developer’s private Telegram channel. The following are transmitted: the text of the message; a rating (for feedback); files attached by the User (up to 5): metadata (including location, device model and capture date) is removed from images and videos before sending; other files, and files that could not be cleaned, are sent unchanged; with the User’s consent, technical details (App and iOS version, language) and, when sent from Bu, the question, the answer and the Bu action log, including any financial information already contained in that chat. Before sending, the User sees the entire content of the report and may switch the technical details off. Feedback on a Bu card (the “Was this helpful?” sheet shown after closing the card) contains the answer Yes or No, the card’s type, its text, which may include amounts and names shown on the screen, an optional comment, the App version and the language. The sheet shows the title of the card the feedback is about; this clause lists the full content of the feedback. By pressing Send, the User consents to this transfer; sending is based on that consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Reports are used solely to correct errors and develop the App. They are stored in the Developer’s database on the Cloudflare platform (Cloudflare D1) for no longer than 24 months from the date they are sent and are then deleted automatically; the copies in the Telegram channel, including attachments, are deleted automatically within 12 months. To group similar reports and prepare summaries for the Developer, the text of a report is processed by Cloudflare’s artificial intelligence models (Workers AI) on Cloudflare’s infrastructure, without being passed to anyone else. The User may delete a sent report at any time under More → Help → My messages; its copy in the Telegram channel is removed at the same time if no more than 48 hours have passed since sending, and otherwise automatically within the period stated above. For this, the App keeps a random deletion key only on the User’s device, and the Developer keeps only its hash. A report may also be deleted at the User’s request quoting its number. Cloudflare, Inc. processes reports on the Developer’s behalf as a processor (clause 4.10). Telegram is an independent service that processes data outside the European Economic Area, including in countries for which the European Commission has not recognised an adequate level of protection, and no standard contractual clauses are concluded with it, so the protection of a report there may be lower than in the European Union. The Developer uses Telegram only to receive notifications of new reports in a private channel. By pressing Send, the User explicitly consents to the report, including its content, being transferred to Telegram for this purpose (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR). Telegram processes the report under its own privacy policy. Where this channel is unavailable or not chosen, the report is sent by email or through the system Share sheet. Technical details may also include the name of the current screen and the most recent actions in the App (names and internal identifiers, without amounts or text); they are governed by the same switch and shown in the preview before sending. Attached files, including bank statements and other documents the User attaches, are transmitted with their content (amounts, counterparties, account and card numbers); each file may be up to 10 MB.

4.12. Sign in with Apple — optional, at the User’s choice. Apple gives the App an identifier of the User that is constant across the Developer’s apps and, on the first sign-in, also the name the User chose to share; the email address is not requested. The name and identifier are stored only on the device in the iOS Keychain and, where iCloud Keychain is turned on, synchronised by Apple to the User’s other devices with end-to-end encryption, so that signing in on another device opens the same profile; the name and identifier are also included in backups (clause 5.4). The User may optionally add a profile photo from the iOS photo library; the App receives only the selected image, reduces it without its metadata and stores it in the same way as the name. The name is shown only in the App and is sent neither to Bu (clause 4.8) nor to the Developer. So that the sign-in can be revoked, the one-time code Apple issues at sign-in is sent to the Developer’s server on the Cloudflare Workers platform (clause 4.11); the server exchanges it with Apple for a token and returns the token to the App, which keeps it in the iOS Keychain on that device only. When the profile is deleted, the token (or a new one-time code) is sent to the same server to revoke the sign-in with Apple. The server does not store or log the code, the token or the identifier. The User can sign out of the profile or delete it under More → Profile; deleting the profile revokes Sign in with Apple where possible and erases the name on all devices, while the App’s financial data stay on the device; their copy in iCloud (clause 5.6) is kept. The User can also stop using Sign in with Apple at any time in the iOS settings, after which the App signs out. Apple Inc. processes the sign-in under its own privacy policy. Legal basis: performance of a function chosen by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4.13. Sign in with Google — optional, at the User’s choice, as an alternative to Sign in with Apple (clause 4.12). The sign-in takes place on Google’s page in the iOS system sign-in window; the App does not use Google’s software libraries. The App requests only the “openid” scope and receives from Google only the persistent identifier of the User’s Google account; the name, email address and photo of the Google account are not requested and are not passed to the App. The one-time authorisation code is exchanged for a token directly between the device and Google (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE), without the Developer’s servers. The identifier and the token are stored in the iOS Keychain on that device only; the token is not included in backups and is used solely to revoke the sign-in. A profile photo, if the User adds one (clause 4.12), is stored in the same way as with Sign in with Apple, together with the identifier: it is synchronised through iCloud Keychain and included in backups (clause 5.4). The identifier and the photo are sent neither to Bu (clause 4.8) nor to the Developer. The User can sign out of the profile or delete it under More → Profile. Signing out makes the device forget the identifier and the token without revoking the sign-in with Google. Deleting the profile sends the token directly to Google to revoke the sign-in and erases the profile photo on all devices; if Google does not respond, the profile is deleted from the device and the User can turn the sign-in off in the Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com, Security section). The App’s financial data and their copy in iCloud (clause 5.6) are not affected. Google LLC (for Users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited) processes the sign-in, including the IP address and Google account information, under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Legal basis: performance of a function chosen by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. iOS features

5.1. Face ID and passcode verification is performed by iOS. The App receives only the result of verification and has no access to biometric data.

5.2. Reminders are implemented as local notifications generated on the device. No push notification server is used.

5.3. Payment details (amount, merchant, card) are provided to the App by iOS on the device through an automation configured by the User in the Shortcuts app.

5.4. Backups are written to a folder selected by the User in the Files app (including iCloud Drive). Storage of backups is governed by the terms of Apple or another storage provider. Backups are encrypted by the App (AES-256-GCM) with the encryption key described in clause 3.5. A backup can be opened on a device where that key is available (including through iCloud Keychain) or with the backup password, if one was set when the backup was made. Without the key or the password a backup cannot be opened, and the Developer cannot recover it. Backups made by versions of the App released before encryption was introduced are not encrypted.

5.5. CSV export is performed through the standard system Share sheet; the recipient is determined by the User.

5.6. iCloud sync (iOS 17 and later). If the device is signed in to iCloud and the User has not turned off Keep in iCloud under More → Data and security, the information listed in clause 3.1 is stored in the User’s private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) and passed to the User’s other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Signing in to a profile in the App (clauses 4.12 and 4.13) is not required for this: all devices with one Apple Account keep one shared copy. Before it is sent, each record is encrypted on the device with the key described in clause 3.5 (AES-256-GCM); record names do not reveal their contents. The Developer has no access to the User’s private iCloud database or to the encryption key. Bu history, the tokens and keys of third-party services, exchange rates and prices, lock and notification settings, and Shortcuts automations are not synchronised. The device learns of changes in iCloud through Apple service notifications that carry no content. The data take up space in the User’s iCloud storage, which is governed by Apple’s terms. When turning the function off, the User chooses whether to keep or erase the copy in iCloud; signing out of the profile or deleting it neither stops synchronisation nor erases the copy. The copy is erased by the Delete all my data function (clause 9.5). Legal basis: performance of a function chosen by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5.7. Voice input in Bu. When the User presses the microphone in the Bu chat, speech is recognised by iOS on the device only; the App asks for access to the microphone and to speech recognition through the system dialogs. The audio is neither stored nor transmitted. The recognised text is placed in the message field and is sent to Bu only when the User sends the message (clause 4.8). Where on-device recognition is not available for the language, the App does not offer voice input; dictation on the iOS keyboard, if the User uses it, is governed by Apple’s terms.

6. Apple diagnostics and TestFlight

6.1. Where “Share With App Developers” is enabled in iOS settings, Apple provides the Developer with anonymised crash reports and aggregate usage statistics. Such data is used solely to correct errors.

6.2. Where the User participates in testing through TestFlight, Apple provides the Developer with the data transmitted by TestFlight: the tester’s name and email address, feedback, screenshots and crash reports. Such data is used solely for testing purposes and is not disclosed to third parties.

6.3. Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement permits developers to collect technical data about the device. Except as set out in this section, the Developer does not exercise that right.

7. Correspondence

7.1. When contacted by email, the Developer receives the sender’s address and the content of the message. Such data is used solely to respond and is deleted no later than 24 months after the correspondence ends.

8. Legal basis for processing

8.1. Processing on the device is carried out to perform the features used by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Requests to third-party services are sent only on the User’s initiative and cease when the corresponding feature is disabled.

8.2. Apple diagnostics and User correspondence are processed on the basis of the Developer’s legitimate interest in correcting errors and responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8.3. Requests to Bu, bug reports and feedback are transmitted on the basis of the User’s consent (clauses 4.9 and 4.11); the Bu server records are processed on the bases stated in clause 4.9; Sign in with Apple and Google on the bases stated in clauses 4.12 and 4.13.

9. Retention and deletion

9.1. Information is stored on the device for as long as the App is installed and is deleted together with it.

9.2. Data in the iOS Keychain may persist after the App is deleted. The monobank token and the keys of third-party services are removed by the Delete all my data function (clause 9.5), which the User is advised to use before deleting the App. The encryption key (clause 3.5) remains in the iOS Keychain after that as well.

9.3. Backups are deleted by the User in the Files app.

9.4. The User may revoke the monobank token at any time at api.monobank.ua.

9.5. The Delete all my data function removes from the device all App data, the tokens and keys of third-party services in the iOS Keychain, Bu history and notifications, both scheduled and delivered. The encryption key (clause 3.5) is intentionally kept in the iOS Keychain so that backups made before the deletion can still be opened. The App does not display this key and offers no function to delete it; it is removed only together with the contents of the Keychain, on a reset of the device and, where the key has been synchronised, on a reset of iCloud Keychain. Backups in the folder selected by the User are not deleted. The function also deletes the profile (clauses 4.12 and 4.13): Sign in with Apple or Google is revoked where possible and the name and profile photo are erased from the iOS Keychain. If iCloud sync is on (clause 5.6), the function also erases the copy of the data in iCloud; without a network connection, the copy is erased the next time the App starts with one. The function does not delete the records on the Bu server (clause 4.8), so that bought questions are not lost; they are deleted within the periods stated in clause 4.8 or at the User’s request (Section 10).

10. User rights

10.1. The User may at any time view, rectify, export (CSV, backup) and delete their information by means of the App. The Developer holds no copy of the information stored in the App. Requests for access to or deletion of correspondence, bug reports and TestFlight data shall be sent to the email address stated in Section 2. The User has the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of their country. The same applies to the Bu server records (clause 4.8). They are tied to a device key and not to the User’s name or email address, so the Developer may ask for information that links them to the User, such as the order number from the Apple receipt; records that cannot be linked to the User are deleted within the periods stated in clause 4.8 (Art. 11 GDPR).

11. Security

11.1. The information listed in clause 3.5 and backups are stored in encrypted form (clauses 3.5 and 5.4); confidential data is stored in the iOS Keychain; network requests are made over HTTPS; access to the App may be restricted by Face ID or passcode (More → Data and security). The protection of information on the device also depends on the device passcode.

11.2. The Developer holds no information from the App other than bug reports and feedback voluntarily sent (clause 4.11) and the Bu server records without the content of chats (clause 4.8). Accordingly, an incident on the Developer’s side cannot result in the disclosure of the financial records stored in the App, other than those the User has included in a report and those passing through the server in transit as part of Bu requests (clause 4.8). The safety of information depends on the protection of the User’s device and of the locations where backups are kept.

11.3. Neither Sign in with Apple nor Sign in with Google gives the Developer the User’s email address (clauses 4.12 and 4.13), and the Developer holds no email addresses of Users other than those known from correspondence. In the event of an incident affecting bug reports, feedback or the Bu server records (clauses 4.8 and 4.11), the Developer will notify the Users concerned by a message in the App and, where their email address is known from correspondence, also by email, and will additionally mention it in the App Store description of an App update, within the time limits set by applicable law.
