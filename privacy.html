Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

1. General provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) sets out how information of users (the “User”) of the BudgetBull mobile application (the “App”) is processed.

1.2. The App is intended for personal finance tracking. No account is required: the User may optionally sign in with Apple (clause 4.12). The App does not use servers operated by the Developer (other than the service receiving bug reports and feedback, clause 4.11, which also revokes Sign in with Apple, clause 4.12) and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Information entered by the User is stored on the User’s device (and, with iCloud sync, also in the User’s private iCloud database, in encrypted form only, clause 5.6) and is not transmitted to the Developer, except for information the User voluntarily sends in a bug report.

1.3. The Developer does not sell or share User information with third parties, does not use it for advertising purposes and does not track the User within the meaning of App Tracking Transparency.

2. Developer

2.1. Developer of the App: Vitalii Prokopenko. Postal address: —. Email address: budgetbull.support@gmail.com.

2.2. With respect to information stored on the User’s device, the Developer is not a controller within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), as the Developer does not receive, store or process such information.

3. Information stored on the device

3.1. Transactions, categories, budgets, goals, events, debt obligations, tax parameters, asset records and App settings are stored in the App’s local storage on the User’s device.

3.2. The personal monobank token and price-feed API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, accessible only on that device and only while it is unlocked. Such data is not included in backups or export files. The key that encrypts App data is stored as described in clause 3.5.

3.3. Transaction data received through a Wallet automation in the Shortcuts app is stored in the App’s local storage until processed by the User.

3.4. Receipt images are processed on the device by Apple frameworks (VisionKit, Vision and, where available, Apple Intelligence with an on-device language model). The App retains only the fields confirmed by the User; the image is neither stored nor transmitted.

3.5. The information listed in clause 3.1 (except certain settings: reminders, App lock and appearance) and Bu history are stored on the device in encrypted form (AES-256-GCM, Apple CryptoKit). The encryption key is generated on the device and stored in the iOS Keychain as a synchronised item: when iCloud Keychain is turned on, it is transferred to the User’s other devices with Apple’s end-to-end encryption. The Developer does not receive the encryption key. A backup password, if set by the User, is stored neither on the device nor in the backups: they hold only a copy of the encryption key, encrypted with a key derived from that password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600,000 iterations).

4. Disclosure to third parties

4.1. The App sends requests only to the third-party services listed below and only when the User uses the corresponding features. Each request discloses to the service the User’s IP address and the technical parameters of the request, which the service processes under its own privacy policy. No such data is transmitted to the Developer.

4.2. monobank (JSC Universal Bank) — where the User connects a personal token. The App requests account and statement data directly from the bank. The token is issued and revoked by the User at api.monobank.ua.

4.3. The price feeds CoinMarketCap, Twelve Data and Finnhub — where the User provides their own key. The key and the tickers or identifiers of assets are transmitted. The quantity and value of the User’s holdings are not transmitted. For the net worth history chart, the App additionally obtains daily prices: for cryptocurrencies from Binance’s public API without a key, and for US stocks from Twelve Data with the User’s key; in both cases only the ticker is transmitted. Cryptocurrency prices are also requested from Binance (data-api.binance.vision, with api.binance.com as a fallback) without an account or key; only the ticker is transmitted. Current and daily prices of cryptocurrencies not listed on Binance are likewise requested from Bybit (api.bybit.com, with api.bytick.com as a fallback) and OKX (www.okx.com) without an account or key; only the ticker is transmitted.

4.4. The logo services Elbstream, Logo.dev (bank logos) and jsDelivr (cryptocurrency icons). The request contains the asset’s ticker or the bank’s website address.

4.5. Central banks — to obtain official exchange rates and official prices of precious metals. Exchange rates are requested from the central bank of the User’s country of residence as set in the App, where the App supports that bank: Narodowy Bank Polski (api.nbp.pl) for Poland, the European Central Bank (data-api.ecb.europa.eu) for the euro area, the National Bank of Georgia (nbg.gov.ge) for Georgia, and the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua) solely for Users resident in Ukraine. Otherwise, or where that bank does not publish the required rate, rates are requested from the bank of the display currency, then from the European Central Bank, then from Narodowy Bank Polski for currencies the European Central Bank does not publish, and finally from the National Bank of Georgia (nbg.gov.ge) for the Georgian lari and the Kazakhstani tenge. Prices of precious metals are obtained from the National Bank of Ukraine for Users resident in Ukraine; for all other Users, the price of gold is obtained from Narodowy Bank Polski. The request contains only a date and a currency code and no personal data. In addition, to check the device clock the App uses the Date header of the responses of the same central bank of the User’s country of residence (the European Central Bank where the App does not support that bank) and a separate HEAD request to it, so that a month is not closed on a wrong clock; these requests contain no personal data.

4.6. Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation) — to obtain a description of a merchant when a receipt is scanned. Only the merchant’s name is transmitted; the amount, the items purchased and any information about the User are not.

4.7. Certain services listed in this section are located outside the European Economic Area and process the IP address under safeguards they determine themselves. Such requests are made by the User directly; the Developer is not a party to them.

4.8. OpenRouter (OpenRouter, Inc.) and the language model providers to which OpenRouter routes a request — where the User enables the feature Bu, the in-app AI assistant, with their own OpenRouter account. The following are transmitted: the User’s questions and messages; a system instruction (current date, currency, list of categories and income sources); data the App provides to the model at its request in order to answer or perform an action — aggregate figures and individual records (transactions with date, amount, category and merchant, goals, events, debts, assets); the User’s ratings of earlier answers together with excerpts of the related questions and answers. Receipt images and tokens or keys for other services are not transmitted. For operations the App recorded on its own from bank notifications or Wallet and that the User has not yet opened or changed, the merchant and note are not transmitted. By default, requests go only to providers that do not retain them or use them for training. Free models, whose providers may retain requests and use them for training under their own terms, are used only after the User turns them on in the Bu settings and confirms it. When Bu is opened, the model also receives the name of the screen it was opened from, the internal identifier of the open record (a transaction, goal, debt or month) and up to the 10 most recent actions in the App — their names only, without amounts or text.

4.9. Data is transmitted to OpenRouter only after the User’s explicit consent given in the App (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Consent may be withdrawn at any time in the Bu settings, whereupon the OpenRouter key, chat history and answer ratings are deleted from the device and transmission ceases. Chat history (up to the 100 most recent messages) and answer ratings are kept in the App’s local storage until deleted by the User, consent is withdrawn or all data is deleted. Sign-in to OpenRouter takes place in the system authentication window; the return to the App passes through the page budgetbull.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives a one-time authorisation code and the IP address and does not store them.

4.10. OpenRouter, Inc., the language model providers and GitHub, Inc. are independent third parties. The Developer is not affiliated with them, is not a party to the agreement between the User and OpenRouter, and does not control their processing of data, which is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

4.11. Bug reports and feedback the User sends through the Write to me form (including from Bu and after shaking the phone) and the app rating form are received by a server operated for the Developer on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.), which stores them and posts a notification to the Developer’s private Telegram channel. The following are transmitted: the text of the message; a rating (for feedback); files attached by the User (up to 5): metadata (including location, device model and capture date) is removed from images and videos before sending; other files, and files that could not be cleaned, are sent unchanged; with the User’s consent, technical details (App and iOS version, language) and, when sent from Bu, the question, the answer and the Bu action log, including any financial information already contained in that chat. Before sending, the User sees the entire content of the report and may switch the technical details off. Feedback on a Bu card (the “Was this helpful?” sheet shown after closing the card) contains the answer Yes or No, the card’s type, its text, which may include amounts and names shown on the screen, an optional comment, the App version and the language. The sheet shows the title of the card the feedback is about; this clause lists the full content of the feedback. By pressing Send, the User consents to this transfer; sending is based on that consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Reports are used solely to correct errors and develop the App. They are stored in the Developer’s database on the Cloudflare platform (Cloudflare D1) for no longer than 24 months from the date they are sent and are then deleted automatically; the copies in the Telegram channel, including attachments, are deleted automatically within 12 months. To group similar reports and prepare summaries for the Developer, the text of a report is processed by Cloudflare’s artificial intelligence models (Workers AI) on Cloudflare’s infrastructure, without being passed to anyone else. The User may delete a sent report at any time under More → Help → My messages; its copy in the Telegram channel is removed at the same time if no more than 48 hours have passed since sending, and otherwise automatically within the period stated above. For this, the App keeps a random deletion key only on the User’s device, and the Developer keeps only its hash. A report may also be deleted at the User’s request quoting its number. Cloudflare, Inc. and Telegram are independent third parties and process the IP address and the content of the report while transmitting and storing it under their own policies. Where this channel is unavailable or not chosen, the report is sent by email or through the system Share sheet. Technical details may also include the name of the current screen and the most recent actions in the App (names and internal identifiers, without amounts or text); they are governed by the same switch and shown in the preview before sending. Attached files, including bank statements and other documents the User attaches, are transmitted with their content (amounts, counterparties, account and card numbers); each file may be up to 10 MB.

4.12. Sign in with Apple — optional, at the User’s choice. Apple gives the App an identifier of the User that is constant across the Developer’s apps and, on the first sign-in, also the name the User chose to share; the email address is not requested. The name and identifier are stored only on the device in the iOS Keychain and, where iCloud Keychain is turned on, synchronised by Apple to the User’s other devices with end-to-end encryption, so that signing in on another device opens the same profile; the name and identifier are also included in backups (clause 5.4). The User may optionally add a profile photo from the iOS photo library; the App receives only the selected image, reduces it without its metadata and stores it in the same way as the name. The name is shown only in the App and is sent neither to Bu (clause 4.8) nor to the Developer. So that the sign-in can be revoked, the one-time code Apple issues at sign-in is sent to the Developer’s server on the Cloudflare Workers platform (clause 4.11); the server exchanges it with Apple for a token and returns the token to the App, which keeps it in the iOS Keychain on that device only. When the profile is deleted, the token (or a new one-time code) is sent to the same server to revoke the sign-in with Apple. The server does not store or log the code, the token or the identifier. The User can sign out of the profile or delete it under More → Profile; deleting the profile revokes Sign in with Apple where possible and erases the name on all devices, while the App’s financial data stay on the device; their copy in iCloud (clause 5.6) is erased. The User can also stop using Sign in with Apple at any time in the iOS settings, after which the App signs out. Apple Inc. processes the sign-in under its own privacy policy. Legal basis: performance of a function chosen by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. iOS features

5.1. Face ID and passcode verification is performed by iOS. The App receives only the result of verification and has no access to biometric data.

5.2. Reminders are implemented as local notifications generated on the device. No push notification server is used.

5.3. Payment details (amount, merchant, card) are provided to the App by iOS on the device through an automation configured by the User in the Shortcuts app.

5.4. Backups are written to a folder selected by the User in the Files app (including iCloud Drive). Storage of backups is governed by the terms of Apple or another storage provider. Backups are encrypted by the App (AES-256-GCM) with the encryption key described in clause 3.5. A backup can be opened on a device where that key is available (including through iCloud Keychain) or with the backup password, if one was set when the backup was made. Without the key or the password a backup cannot be opened, and the Developer cannot recover it. Backups made by versions of the App released before encryption was introduced are not encrypted.

5.5. CSV export is performed through the standard system Share sheet; the recipient is determined by the User.

5.6. iCloud sync (iOS 17 and later). If the User has signed in with Apple (clause 4.12) and has not turned off Keep in iCloud under More → Profile, the information listed in clause 3.1 is stored in the User’s private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) and passed to the User’s other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Before it is sent, each record is encrypted on the device with the key described in clause 3.5 (AES-256-GCM); record names do not reveal their contents. The Developer has no access to the User’s private iCloud database or to the encryption key. Bu history, the tokens and keys of third-party services, exchange rates and prices, lock and notification settings, and Shortcuts automations are not synchronised. The device learns of changes in iCloud through Apple service notifications that carry no content. The data take up space in the User’s iCloud storage, which is governed by Apple’s terms. When turning the function off, the User chooses whether to keep or erase the copy in iCloud; signing out of the profile stops synchronisation and keeps the copy. The copy is erased when the profile is deleted (clause 4.12) and by the Delete all my data function (clause 9.5). Legal basis: performance of a function chosen by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Apple diagnostics and TestFlight

6.1. Where “Share With App Developers” is enabled in iOS settings, Apple provides the Developer with anonymised crash reports and aggregate usage statistics. Such data is used solely to correct errors.

6.2. Where the User participates in testing through TestFlight, Apple provides the Developer with the data transmitted by TestFlight: the tester’s name and email address, feedback, screenshots and crash reports. Such data is used solely for testing purposes and is not disclosed to third parties.

6.3. Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement permits developers to collect technical data about the device. Except as set out in this section, the Developer does not exercise that right.

7. Correspondence

7.1. When contacted by email, the Developer receives the sender’s address and the content of the message. Such data is used solely to respond and is deleted no later than 24 months after the correspondence ends.

8. Legal basis for processing

8.1. Processing on the device is carried out to perform the features used by the User (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Requests to third-party services are sent only on the User’s initiative and cease when the corresponding feature is disabled.

8.2. Apple diagnostics and User correspondence are processed on the basis of the Developer’s legitimate interest in correcting errors and responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Retention and deletion

9.1. Information is stored on the device for as long as the App is installed and is deleted together with it.

9.2. Data in the iOS Keychain may persist after the App is deleted. The monobank token and the keys of third-party services are removed by the Delete all my data function (clause 9.5), which the User is advised to use before deleting the App. The encryption key (clause 3.5) remains in the iOS Keychain after that as well.

9.3. Backups are deleted by the User in the Files app.

9.4. The User may revoke the monobank token at any time at api.monobank.ua.

9.5. The Delete all my data function removes from the device all App data, the tokens and keys of third-party services in the iOS Keychain, Bu history and notifications, both scheduled and delivered. The encryption key (clause 3.5) is intentionally kept in the iOS Keychain so that backups made before the deletion can still be opened. The App does not display this key and offers no function to delete it; it is removed only together with the contents of the Keychain, on a reset of the device and, where the key has been synchronised, on a reset of iCloud Keychain. Backups in the folder selected by the User are not deleted. The function also deletes the profile (clause 4.12): Sign in with Apple is revoked where possible and the name is erased from the iOS Keychain. If iCloud sync is on (clause 5.6), the function also erases the copy of the data in iCloud; without a network connection, the copy is erased the next time the App starts with one.

The protection of information on the device also depends on the device passcode.

11.2. The Developer holds no information from the App other than bug reports and feedback voluntarily sent (clause 4.11), which are stored on the Developer’s server and in its Telegram channel. Accordingly, an incident on the Developer’s side cannot result in the disclosure of any other information from the App. The safety of information depends on the protection of the User’s device and of the locations where backups are kept.

11.3. Sign in with Apple does not give the Developer the User’s email address (clause 4.12), and the Developer holds no email addresses of Users other than those known from correspondence. In the event of an incident affecting bug reports and feedback (clause 4.11), the Developer will notify Users in the App Store description of an App update and by a message in the App, and Users whose email address is known from correspondence also by email, within the time limits set by applicable law.
